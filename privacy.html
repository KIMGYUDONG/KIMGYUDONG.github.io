Privacy Policy (gws-cli)

Last updated: 2026-10-03

gws-cli is a personal command-line setup used only by its owner (zlsrbrhd@gmail.com) to access the owner's own Google Workspace data. It is not offered to other users.

Data use

  • Google user data is accessed only on the owner's own computer, at the owner's request.
  • No data is collected, stored on any server, sold, or shared with third parties.
  • OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
